Module 01

Failure Theories, Static/Dynamic Loading & Fatigue

Theoretical stress concentration ($K_t = \frac{\sigma_{\text{max}}}{\sigma_{\text{nom}}}$, circular hole $K_t=3$), brittle vs ductile static behavior, S-N diagram & endurance limit asymptote ($10^6$ cycles for steel), fluctuating load criteria (Soderberg $\frac{\sigma_a}{S_e}+\frac{\sigma_m}{S_{yt}}=\frac{1}{N}$, Goodman $\frac{\sigma_a}{S_e}+\frac{\sigma_m}{S_{ut}}=\frac{1}{N}$, Gerber parabola), notch sensitivity ($K_f = 1 + q(K_t-1)$), endurance modifiers ($S_e = k_a k_b k_c k_d k_e S_e'$), shot peening, and Griffith fracture strength ($\sigma_f = \sqrt{\frac{2E\gamma}{\pi c}}$).

$$K_t = \frac{\sigma_{\max}}{\sigma_{\text{nom}}}, \quad \frac{\sigma_a}{S_e} + \frac{\sigma_m}{S_{yt}} = \frac{1}{N}, \quad K_f = 1 + q(K_t - 1), \quad S_e = k_a k_b k_c k_d k_e S_e'$$

Module 02

Threaded Joints, Power Screws, Welded & Riveted Joints

Thread forms (square 0°, Acme 29°, buttress 1-way power, metric 60°), self-locking ($\phi \ge \alpha \implies \eta \le 50\%$), effort ($P = W\tan(\alpha+\phi)$), screw jack max efficiency ($\eta_{\text{max}} = \frac{1-\sin\phi}{1+\sin\phi}$), V-thread equivalent friction ($\mu' = \frac{\mu}{\cos(\beta/2)}$), bolt core tensile design ($d_c = \sqrt{\frac{4P}{\pi\sigma_t}}$), bolt of uniform strength resilience ($U = \frac{\sigma^2 V}{2E}$), turnbuckles, studs, fillet weld throat ($t = 0.707 h$, weakest in shear), transverse vs parallel fillet, butt weld size ($t$), Unwin's formula ($d = 6\sqrt{t}$), margin ($m = 1.5d$), tearing efficiency ($\eta_t = \frac{p-d}{p}$), and caulking/fullering leak-proofing.

$$\phi \ge \alpha, \quad \eta_{\text{max}} = \frac{1-\sin\phi}{1+\sin\phi}, \quad d_c = \sqrt{\frac{4P}{\pi\sigma_t}}, \quad t = 0.707h, \quad d = 6\sqrt{t}, \quad \eta_t = \frac{p-d}{p}$$

Module 03

Design of Bearings, Brakes & Clutches

Rolling contact bearings ($L_{10} = (C/P_e)^k \cdot 10^6$, load-life relation $L \propto 1/P^3$, equivalent load $P_e = X F_r + Y F_a$, bore $= \text{code} \times 5$), journal bearings (hydrodynamic wedge, hydrostatic pump, Petroff's equation $f = \frac{2\pi^2\mu N}{P}\frac{r}{c}$, $ZN/P$, clearance $1 \mu\text{m/mm}$, $L/D = 1-2$), long shoe brakes ($2\theta > 60^\circ \implies \mu_{\text{eq}} = \frac{4\mu\sin\theta}{2\theta+\sin 2\theta}$, torque $T = \mu_{\text{eq}} R_n R$, internal expanding shoe automotive brakes), and friction clutches (uniform wear theory $T = \mu W R_m$ for safe design, uniform pressure theory $T = \frac{2}{3}\mu W \frac{r_1^3-r_2^3}{r_1^2-r_2^2}$, multi-plate contact pairs $n_1+n_2-1$, centrifugal clutch $F_{\text{cf}} - F_{\text{spring}}$).

$$L \propto \frac{1}{P^3}, \quad f = \frac{2\pi^2 \mu N}{P}\left(\frac{r}{c}\right), \quad \mu_{\text{eq}} = \frac{4\mu \sin\theta}{2\theta + \sin 2\theta}, \quad T = \mu W R_m, \quad n = n_1 + n_2 - 1$$

Module 04

Design of Shafts, Keys, Cotter Joints & Couplings

Solid and hollow shaft torsion ($\tau = \frac{16T}{\pi d^3}$, $\tau = \frac{16T}{\pi d_o^3(1-k^4)}$, hollow shaft superior strength/weight), combined bending and torsion ($T_e = \sqrt{M^2+T^2}$, $M_e = \frac{1}{2}[M+\sqrt{M^2+T^2}]$, $\tau_{\text{max}} = \frac{16 T_e}{\pi d^3}$), key design ($\sigma_c = 2\tau$ for square key, Woodruff self-aligning for tapered shafts, Feather axial sliding, Kennedy 90° heavy duty, Saddle friction), cotter joints (co-axial rods under axial load, single taper 1 in 24 to 1 in 48, thickness $t \approx 0.31d$, piston rod to crosshead), and couplings (muff sleeve hollow shaft, flange reamed bolts, bushed pin flexible, Oldham's for parallel offset shafts, Hooke's/Universal joint for intersecting angular shafts).

$$\tau = \frac{16T}{\pi d^3}, \quad T_e = \sqrt{M^2 + T^2}, \quad M_e = \frac{1}{2}\left[M + \sqrt{M^2 + T^2}\right], \quad \sigma_c = 2\tau, \quad t \approx 0.31d$$

Module 05

Design of Mechanical Springs

Helical compression and tension springs ($C = D/d$, Wahl factor $K = \frac{4C-1}{4C-4} + \frac{0.615}{C}$, max inner surface shear stress $\tau_{\text{max}} = K \frac{8PD}{\pi d^3}$, deflection $\delta = \frac{8PD^3N}{Gd^4}$, stiffness $k = \frac{Gd^4}{8D^3N}$), spring combinations (series $1/k_{\text{eq}} = 1/k_1 + 1/k_2$, parallel $k_{\text{eq}} = k_1 + k_2$, cutting spring in half $k' = 2k$), strain energy ($U/V = \tau^2/4G$), concentric helical springs (opposite winding hand), leaf springs (master vs graduated leaves, un-nipped stress $\sigma_f = 1.5\sigma_g$, nipping equalizes stresses $\sigma_f = \sigma_g$), Belleville disc springs (high load in small axial space), and spring surge ($f_n = \frac{1}{2}\sqrt{k/m}$).

$$C = \frac{D}{d}, \quad K = \frac{4C-1}{4C-4} + \frac{0.615}{C}, \quad \tau_{\text{max}} = K \frac{8PD}{\pi d^3}, \quad k = \frac{Gd^4}{8D^3N}, \quad \sigma_f = \sigma_g \text{ (Nipping)}$$
