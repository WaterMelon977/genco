Slider-Crank Mechanism Dynamics
High-Yield Exam Revision Guide | APGENCO AE • GATE • SSC JE

1. Piston Kinematics

Connecting rod obliquity ratio $n = \frac{l}{r}$. As $n \to \infty$, piston motion becomes pure Simple Harmonic Motion (SHM).

$$\text{Connecting Rod Obliquity Angle: } \sin\phi = \frac{\sin\theta}{n}$$
$$\text{Piston Displacement from IDC: } x = r \left[ (1 - \cos\theta) + \frac{\sin^2\theta}{2n} \right]$$
$$\text{Piston Velocity: } v_p = \omega r \left( \sin\theta + \frac{\sin 2\theta}{2n} \right)$$
$$\text{Piston Acceleration: } a_p = \omega^2 r \left( \cos\theta + \frac{\cos 2\theta}{n} \right)$$
$r$: crank radius ($\text{m}$) $l$: connecting rod length ($\text{m}$) $n = l/r$: obliquity ratio ($3.5 - 5$)
⚠️ EXAM TRAP — Acceleration Extremes:
  • At IDC ($\theta = 0^\circ$): $a_p = \omega^2 r \left(1 + \frac{1}{n}\right)$ — Maximum acceleration towards crank!
  • At ODC ($\theta = 180^\circ$): $a_p = -\omega^2 r \left(1 - \frac{1}{n}\right)$ — Maximum deceleration!
  • At Dead Centres: Velocity $v_p = 0$, but Acceleration $a_p \neq 0$!

2. Dynamic Force Analysis & Turning Moment

$$\text{Inertia Force: } F_i = m_{\text{rec}} \, \omega^2 r \left(\cos\theta + \frac{\cos 2\theta}{n}\right)$$
$$\text{Net Piston Effort: } F_p = F_{\text{gas}} - F_i \mp R_f \pm m_{\text{rec}} g$$
$$\text{Crank Effort (Turning Moment): } T = F_p \cdot r \left(\sin\theta + \frac{\sin 2\theta}{2n}\right)$$
$F_p$: net piston effort ($\text{N}$) Side thrust on cylinder wall: $N_s = F_p \tan\phi$ Force along connecting rod: $F_q = \frac{F_p}{\cos\phi}$

3. Dynamically Equivalent Two-Mass System

To replace a connecting rod of mass $m_{\text{conrod}}$ by two point masses $m_1$ (at gudgeon pin) and $m_2$ (at crank pin), 3 conditions must hold:

$$m_1 + m_2 = m_{\text{conrod}}, \qquad m_1 l_1 = m_2 l_2, \qquad m_1 l_1^2 + m_2 l_2^2 = I_G$$

4. Primary & Secondary Unbalanced Forces

$$\text{Primary Unbalanced Force (at frequency } \omega\text{): } F_P = m_{\text{rec}} r \omega^2 \cos\theta$$
$$\text{Secondary Unbalanced Force (at frequency } 2\omega\text{): } F_S = \frac{m_{\text{rec}} r \omega^2}{n} \cos 2\theta$$
🔴 Single-Cylinder Balancing Rule: Complete balance of a single-cylinder reciprocating engine using rotating masses is IMPOSSIBLE. Balancing primary force along the stroke creates an equal unbalanced force perpendicular to the stroke!

5. Locomotive Balancing Effects (Partial Balance Fraction $c \approx 2/3$)

Effect Formula Peak Crank Angle $\theta$
Hammer Blow (Vertical rail force) $H = B \cdot b \cdot \omega^2 = c \cdot m_{\text{rec}} \cdot r \cdot \omega^2$ When balance weight is vertical ($\theta = 90^\circ, 270^\circ$)
Tractive Effort Variation ($\Delta F_T$) $\Delta F_T = \pm \sqrt{2} (1 - c) m_{\text{rec}} r \omega^2$ $\theta = 135^\circ$ and $315^\circ$
Swaying Couple ($C_{\text{sway}}$) $C_{\text{sway}} = \pm \frac{a}{\sqrt{2}} (1 - c) m_{\text{rec}} r \omega^2$ $\theta = 45^\circ$ and $225^\circ$
⚠️ Hammer Blow & Rail Lift: $$\text{Wheel Lifts Off Rail when: } H \ge W_{\text{static}}$$ Hammer blow grows with $\omega^2$ ($N^2$), setting the maximum safe speed limit for steam locomotives!

6. Key Points to Remember

Exam Survival Checklist
  • Obliquity ratio: $n = l/r$ ($3.5 - 5$). Pure SHM when $n \to \infty$.
  • IDC Acceleration: $a_p = \omega^2 r (1 + 1/n)$. ODC: $a_p = -\omega^2 r (1 - 1/n)$.
  • Dead Centres: Velocity $v_p = 0$, Turning Moment $T = 0$, Acceleration $a_p \neq 0$.
  • Side thrust: $N_s = F_p \tan\phi$. Conrod force: $F_q = F_p / \cos\phi$.
  • Primary vs Secondary Force: Primary frequency $= \omega$, Secondary frequency $= 2\omega$, magnitude $= \frac{1}{n} F_P$.
  • Single Cylinder: Cannot be 100% balanced with rotating masses alone.
  • Locomotive Balance: Partial balance fraction $c \approx 2/3$. Cranks set at $90^\circ$ for self-starting.
  • Hammer Blow: $H = c \cdot m_{\text{rec}} r \omega^2$. Wheel lifts when $H \ge W_{\text{static}}$.
  • Peak Angles: Tractive Effort Fluctuation $\to 135^\circ / 315^\circ$; Swaying Couple $\to 45^\circ / 225^\circ$.
