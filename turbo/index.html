Module 01

Working Principles of Gas Turbines

Ideal Brayton (Joule) cycle, air-standard efficiency ($\eta = 1 - 1/r_p^{(\gamma-1)/\gamma}$), work ratio ($r_w = w_{\text{net}}/w_T$), back-work ratio ($r_{bw} = w_C/w_T \approx 0.4-0.6$), reheating ($w_{\text{net}}\uparrow, \eta\downarrow$), intercooling ($P_i = \sqrt{P_1 P_2}$), regeneration ($q_{\text{in}}\downarrow, \eta\uparrow$), optimum pressure ratio ($r_{p,\text{opt}} = (T_3/T_1)^{\frac{\gamma}{2(\gamma-1)}}$), $T_2 = T_4 = \sqrt{T_1 T_3}$, air-fuel ratio $60:1$, and nickel superalloys.

$$\eta = 1 - \frac{1}{r_p^{(\gamma-1)/\gamma}}, \quad r_w = 1 - \frac{w_C}{w_T}, \quad P_i = \sqrt{P_1 P_2}, \quad r_{p,\text{opt}} = \left(\frac{T_3}{T_1}\right)^{\frac{\gamma}{2(\gamma-1)}}$$

Module 02

Working Principles of Steam Turbines

Impulse vs Reaction ($R = \frac{\Delta h_m}{\Delta h_f + \Delta h_m}$), Parson's 50% reaction turbine ($\rho_{\text{opt}} = \cos\alpha, \eta_{\text{max}} = \frac{2\cos^2\alpha}{1+\cos^2\alpha}$), Curtis velocity compounding ($\rho_{\text{opt}} = \frac{\cos\alpha}{2n}$), Rateau pressure compounding, simple impulse optimums ($\rho_{\text{opt}} = \frac{1}{2}\cos\alpha, \eta_{\text{max}} = \cos^2\alpha$), stage efficiency ($\eta_s = \eta_b \times \eta_N$), steam rate ($3600 / W_{\text{net}}$), back-pressure & pass-out turbines, and critical speed.

$$R = \frac{\Delta h_m}{\Delta h_f + \Delta h_m}, \quad \rho_{\text{opt, Parson}} = \cos\alpha, \quad \rho_{\text{opt, Curtis}} = \frac{\cos\alpha}{2n}, \quad \eta_s = \eta_b \cdot \eta_N, \quad \text{Steam Rate} = \frac{3600}{W_{\text{net}}}$$

Module 03

Rankine Cycle, Modified Rankine & Steam Power Systems

Ideal Rankine efficiency ($\eta_R = \frac{W_T - W_P}{Q_{\text{in}}} = 1 - \frac{T_{\text{cond}}}{T_m}$), pump work ($W_P \approx v_3(P_4-P_3)$), superheating ($\Delta T_{\text{sup}} = T_{\text{sup}}-T_{\text{sat}}$), reheating ($x>0.88$, $RF = 1.02-1.08$), regeneration (bled fraction $y$, deaeration), Modified Rankine (early release, stroke reduction), combustion stoichiometry ($1 \text{ kg C} \implies \frac{7}{3} \text{ kg CO}$), coal ranks, mountings vs accessories, draught systems ($\eta_{\text{natural}} \approx 0.25\%$), and equivalent evaporation ($m_e = \frac{m_s(h-h_{f1})}{2257}$).

$$\eta_R = 1 - \frac{T_{\text{cond}}}{T_m}, \quad y = \frac{h_f - h_{\text{cond}}}{h_{\text{bleed}} - h_{\text{cond}}}, \quad 1 \text{ kg C} \implies \frac{7}{3} \text{ kg CO}, \quad m_e = \frac{m_s (h - h_{f1})}{2257}$$

Module 04

Jet Propulsion & Rocket Systems

Air-breathing propulsive efficiency ($\eta_p = \frac{2u}{u+v}$), rocket efficiency ($\eta_p = \frac{2V_f V_e}{V_e^2 + V_f^2}$, peak $100\%$ at $V_f=V_e$), ship right-angle inlet ($\frac{2Vu}{V^2+u^2}$, max $50\%$), turbojet thrust ($F=\dot{m}(V_{\text{exit}}-V_{\text{inlet}})$), rocket thrust ($V_{\text{inlet}}=0$), thrust coefficient ($C_F = \frac{F}{P_c A_t}$), turboprop sequence (Propeller $\to$ Compressor $\to$ Burner $\to$ Turbine), ramjet zero static thrust, pulsejet 2-stroke analogue, hybrid rockets ($\text{LiH}+\text{ClF}_3$), specific impulse ($I_{sp} = 1/\text{SPC}$), Mach angle ($\mu = \sin^{-1}(1/M)$), and turbojet combustor requirements.

$$\eta_{\text{jet}} = \frac{2u}{u+v}, \quad \eta_{\text{rocket}} = \frac{2 V_f V_e}{V_e^2 + V_f^2}, \quad F = \dot{m}(V_e - V_i), \quad C_F = \frac{F}{P_c A_t}, \quad \mu = \sin^{-1}\left(\frac{1}{M}\right)$$
