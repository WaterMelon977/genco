Fourier's Law
$Q = -kA\,\dfrac{dT}{dx}$
Critical Radius (Cylinder)
$r_c = k/h$
Dittus-Boelter
$Nu = 0.023\,Re^{0.8}\,Pr^n$
LMTD
$\text{LMTD} = \dfrac{\Delta T_1 - \Delta T_2}{\ln(\Delta T_1/\Delta T_2)}$
NTU Definition
$\text{NTU} = UA/C_{\min}$
Stefan-Boltzmann
$E_b = \sigma T^4$
Radiation Shields
$Q_n = Q_0/(n+1)$

$\frac{T-T_\infty}{T_i-T_\infty} = e^{-Bi \cdot Fo}$
Wien's Law
$\lambda_{\max} T = 2898\,\mu\text{m·K}$
Fin Efficiency
$\eta_f = \tanh(mL)/mL$
