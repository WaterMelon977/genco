Module 01

Steady & Unsteady Heat Conduction

Thermal diffusivity ($\alpha = \frac{k}{\rho C_p}$), Fourier's law ($Q = -k A \frac{dT}{dx}$), 3D conduction equations (Laplace $\nabla^2 T = 0$, Poisson $\nabla^2 T + \frac{\dot{q}_g}{k} = 0$), critical radius of insulation ($r_c = \frac{k}{h}$ cylinder, $\frac{2k}{h}$ sphere), radial conduction ($Q_{\text{cyl}}, Q_{\text{sph}}$), composite walls ($k_{\text{eq}} = \frac{2k_1 k_2}{k_1 + k_2}$), internal heat generation ($T_{\text{max}} = T_w + \frac{\dot{q}_g R^2}{6k}$ sphere, $\frac{\dot{q}_g R^2}{4k}$ cylinder), and transient cooling with Newton's law ($\tau = \frac{\rho V C_p}{hA}$).

$$\alpha = \frac{k}{\rho C_p}, \quad r_c = \frac{k}{h}, \quad Q_{\text{cyl}} = \frac{2\pi k L \Delta T}{\ln(r_o/r_i)}, \quad k_{\text{eq}} = \frac{2k_1 k_2}{k_1 + k_2}, \quad T_{\text{max, sph}} = T_w + \frac{\dot{q}_g R^2}{6k}$$

Module 02

Fins, Heat Exchangers, LMTD & NTU Methods

$$m = \sqrt{\frac{hP}{kA_c}}, \quad R_f = \frac{1}{U_d} - \frac{1}{U_c}, \quad \text{LMTD} = \frac{\Delta T_1 - \Delta T_2}{\ln(\Delta T_1/\Delta T_2)}, \quad \text{NTU} = \frac{UA}{C_{\min}}, \quad \varepsilon_{C=0} = 1 - e^{-\text{NTU}}$$

Module 04

Convective Heat Transfer & Boundary Layers

Dimensionless parameters ($Nu = \frac{hL}{k}$, $Pr = \frac{\mu C_p}{k}$, $Re = \frac{\rho VL}{\mu}$, $Gr = \frac{g\beta \Delta T L^3}{\nu^2}$, $Ra = Gr \cdot Pr$, $St = \frac{Nu}{Re \cdot Pr} = \frac{C_f}{2}$, $Bi = \frac{h L_c}{k_{\text{solid}}}$, $Fo = \frac{\alpha t}{L_c^2}$), natural convection velocity profile (zero at wall and outer edge), mixed flow criterion ($0.1 \le \frac{Gr}{Re^2} \le 10$), boundary layer ratio $\frac{\delta_t}{\delta} = Pr^{-1/3}$, local $h_x$ decay downstream, flat plate laminar $\bar{h}_L = 2 h_{x=L}$, Dittus-Boelter pipe equation $Nu = 0.023 Re^{0.8} Pr^n$, infinite fin temp profile $e^{-mx}$, and lumped analysis ($Bi < 0.1$, exponential decay $\exp(-Bi \cdot Fo)$).
