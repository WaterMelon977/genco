Module 01

Free, Damped, Forced Vibrations & Whirling

Free vibrations ($\omega_n = \sqrt{\frac{k}{m}}$), static deflection method ($f_n = \frac{1}{2\pi}\sqrt{\frac{g}{\delta}}$), damping ratio ($\xi = \frac{c}{c_c}$), damped natural frequency ($\omega_d = \omega_n\sqrt{1-\xi^2}$), logarithmic decrement ($\delta = \ln(x_n/x_{n+1}) \approx 2\pi\xi$), magnification factor ($M$), phase angle lag ($90^\circ$ at resonance), vibration isolation & transmissibility ($TR$) for $r > \sqrt{2}$, critical / whirling speeds of shafts ($\omega_c = \sqrt{\frac{g}{\delta}}$), torsional multi-rotor systems ($N_{\text{nodes}} = n-1$), and approximate methods (Dunkerley's formula).

$$\omega_n = \sqrt{\frac{k}{m}}, \quad f_n = \frac{1}{2\pi}\sqrt{\frac{g}{\delta}}, \quad c_c = 2\sqrt{km}, \quad \delta \approx 2\pi\xi, \quad TR = \sqrt{\frac{1+(2\xi r)^2}{(1-r^2)^2+(2\xi r)^2}}, \quad \omega_c = \omega_n$$
